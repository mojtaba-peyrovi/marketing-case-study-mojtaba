Methodology & Process
End-to-end analytical approach, assumptions, and limitations

The dataset contains 1,152 rows × 17 columns covering the full calendar year 2024 across 4 markets and 24 granular channels structured in a 3-level hierarchy (Paid/Non-Paid → Channel Category → Specific Channel).

Key assumption: Rows with UNKNOWN market or channel labels are excluded from main analyses but retained for data quality counting. This affects 25% of rows.

Derived metrics added: cac (Marketing Spend / Subscribers), roas (Revenue Create Date / Spend), conversion_rate (Total Conversions / Subscribers), revenue_per_subscriber.

The dataset provides two revenue columns:

  • New Revenue Create Date — attributed on the day the subscription started (trial day 1). Used for growth trend analysis.
  • New Revenue Invoice Date — attributed on the day the customer first paid (after the 30-day trial). Used for cash-flow analysis.
All ROAS calculations use Create Date revenue to align with subscriber acquisition timing.

The IQR (Interquartile Range) method is used for outlier detection:

Lower = Q1 − 1.5 × IQR
Upper = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

Applied to Marketing Spendings and Subscriber columns independently. This is a non-parametric approach suitable for skewed distributions (marketing data typically has right-skewed distributions).

  • Single year of data — cannot establish seasonality baselines or YoY trends.
  • No customer-level data — all analysis is at channel × month aggregation level.
  • 62% of rows lack conversion data — channel efficiency analysis is limited to the tracked subset.
  • UNKNOWN rows (25%) cannot be attributed to any market or channel hierarchy.
  • CAC is calculated as spend / all subscribers (not just paid-channel subscribers) due to mixed attribution in the dataset — this underestimates true paid CAC.

Privacy & Data Security Statement
Since this analysis involves sensitive subscriber and revenue data, the solution was built fully containerized. All data processing occurs inside the Docker container at build time. No raw data is exposed via web routes at runtime. AI coding agents had zero access to the raw dataset outside the container environment — ensuring 100% data privacy.
