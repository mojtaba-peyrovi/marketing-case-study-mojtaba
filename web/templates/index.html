Trial-to-paid: where intent goes to die (or thrive)

Yearly trial conversion by channel — a quality signal CAC misses.
What this reveals
→ Influencer IG sends the best users.
29% of Influencer IG trials convert to paid — compared to 21% for TikTok and 18% for the English market. That's 8–11% more paying customers for the same number of trial starts.
→ Paid social underperforms organic.
Direct, Word of Mouth, and PR all beat Meta, TikTok, and Google on trial-to-paid.
→ TikTok was the wrong audience.
20.6% — 8pts below Influencer IG. Combined with €70 CAC, the channel was paused after Sep. Confirmed correct call.
→ The English-market funnel is broken.
{{ d.markets[2].t2p_pct }}% — the lowest. Likely root causes: payment method coverage, content depth, paywall friction.
